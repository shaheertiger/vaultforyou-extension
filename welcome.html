Private Bookmarks by VaultForYou

Private bookmarks, kept to yourself.

Save pages into an encrypted vault that opens with a password you choose. Nothing leaves this computer.

  • Encrypted with AES-256 and stored on this computer only
  • No account, no sign-up, no tracking
  • Kept out of Chrome’s regular bookmarks and sync

How to use it

  1. 1
    Pin it to your toolbar. Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Private Bookmarks.
  2. 2
    Save any page. Click the Private Bookmarks button, or press Alt+Shift+B. Pick a folder and save.
  3. 3
    Open your library. Choose “Library” in the popup to search, favorite and organize bookmarks in the side panel.
  4. 4
    Keep a backup. In Settings, export an encrypted backup. There is no password reset, so a backup is your safety net.
